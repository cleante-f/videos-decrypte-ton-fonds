## My positions

1. Fees are the silent killer: a 2 % yearly fee beats almost any "skill" a
   manager claims (Buffett's bet, hedge funds vs. an index fund).
2. A boring, cheap index fund beats most professionals over ten years.
   Throwing darts can beat experts.
3. Time in the market matters more than timing it. Starting small and early
   beats starting big and late.
4. Panic selling, holding losers "to get even", and leaving everything in a
   savings account are the same brain bug, not bad luck.
5. Most famous finance disasters (Ponzi, Madoff, Enron, LTCM, GameStop) come
   from leverage, greed or hidden information, and the small investor is
   usually the one left holding the bag.
